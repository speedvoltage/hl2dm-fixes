HL2DM MATCHMAKING - ACHIEVEMENT PROPOSALS
Draft for review - 5 November 2026

118 active candidates, with proposal IDs 092 and 094 withdrawn after review.
This is a selection pool, not a commitment to ship every candidate. I would start with
roughly 50-60, favoring varied challenges over multiple tiers of the same counter.
Names, targets and difficulty estimates are provisional and should be playtested.
No achievement code, Steam definitions or menu changes are included in this draft.
Implementation is deferred until the mod has its own Steam AppID and the required
matchmaking support. The list remains design work only until then.

READING THE LIST
Each entry has a stable proposal ID, title, difficulty and unlock condition.
Very easy / Easy / Medium / Hard / Very hard describe expected execution difficulty.
Milestone describes accumulated progress; a large counter is not necessarily hard.
A trailing [T] flags extra tracking or attribution that needs implementation and
validation beyond ordinary kill/round events. Unmarked entries still need the
achievement framework; they are not already implemented.

PROPOSED SHARED RULES
- Unless stated otherwise, kill means a credited kill against a human opponent.
  Teammates, bots, suicides and world deaths do not advance combat achievements.
- One life ends on death, switching teams, entering spectator, disconnecting or
  round restart. Timed multikills require distinct victims during that life.
- An airborne kill follows the existing killfeed wings rule. Swimming does not
  count. Headshot kills follow the existing killfeed headshot rule.
- Weapon names mean their normal attack unless an alternate attack is named.
  SLAM satchels and tripmines, SMG grenades and AR2 orbs are named explicitly.
- Distances are server world units, measured between player origins at the kill
  unless an entry specifies launch positions. They are tuning values, not meters.
- Qualifying matches should include normal matchmaking and community DM/TDM.
  Suggested minimums for match/win awards: four active humans in FFA or TDM,
  with at least two per team in TDM; two humans in the designated Duel mode.
  Join before halfway, play at least half the round, and remain through its end.
  A tied result is not a win; admin restarts and aborted rounds are not finishes.
- Cheat-enabled play and developer previews do not unlock gameplay achievements.
  Decide the eligible community-server settings before implementation; physics,
  damage and instant-respawn changes can trivialize some challenges.
- Progress never requires enabling bright models, hitmarkers or hit sounds.
  No rewards for calling kick votes, reporting, muting or changing server rules.
- Any cumulative target below spans qualifying sessions unless stated otherwise.

RECOMMENDED SELECTION APPROACH
==============================
The list intentionally offers alternatives. I would not ship every first-kill
award alongside every weapon counter; choose enough to introduce the weapons
without filling the page with near-identical progress bars.

A possible 60-achievement launch shortlist, using the IDs above:
001, 002, 005, 006, 007, 008, 009, 010, 019, 020,
021, 022, 023, 024, 025, 026, 027, 028, 029, 030,
031, 032, 033, 034, 035, 036, 037, 038, 040, 041,
043, 044, 048, 051, 053, 054, 059, 060, 061, 063,
064, 069, 071, 072, 074, 075, 080, 082, 083, 085,
086, 088, 090, 096, 098, 101, 103, 105, 118, 120.

This shortlist is a design recommendation, not a claim that all its tracking
already exists. In particular, prop returns and projectile multikills need
correct ownership histories before they are safe to award.

Candidates to validate especially carefully:
- 046/047: distinguish an actual interception from picking up a resting prop.
- 048/049: determine who caused the barrel explosion, including chained barrels.
- 050-055, 114/115: correlate victims to one specific projectile or prop launch.
- 058: reserve candidate; reliable around-cover detection is more complex than
  a simple kill counter and must not depend on the client's camera visibility.
- 059/060: preserve original ownership after a gravity-gun catch or deflection.
- 069-073, 077, 113: sample movement state before death changes the victim state.
- 088-091 and 093: assist, trade and timing histories are new achievement tracking, not a
  proposal to bring the removed scoreboard statistics or columns back.
- 107: reserve candidate; ensure the condition is understandable and achievable
  in normal play, not just arranged farming sessions. Proposal 110 now only needs
  the victim's active weapon at the lethal hit.
- 117: establish mutually exclusive damage categories, especially shotgun vs.
  bullet and explosive barrels vs. direct physics impacts.

No time-limited achievements, consecutive-day logins, mandatory purchases,
required friend invitations or achievements for losing/dying deliberately.
Keep achievements cosmetic: no damage, speed, weapon or visibility advantages.
Do not award achievements for ordinary local menu previews or test self-votes.


ACHIEVEMENTS PAGE - PROPOSED DIRECTION
=====================================
Reference: the Black Mesa achievements screenshot supplied in this conversation.
Use its charcoal, orange and white palette within our existing menu theme.

Main-menu entry: ACHIEVEMENTS, opening a dedicated full-page view.
Top: page title, overall unlocked count (for example 18 / 60) and progress bar.
Below: search, All / Locked / Unlocked filters, and category selection.
Sort choices: recommended order, nearest to completion, recently unlocked.
Main area: two columns of cards when there is enough room; one at narrow sizes.
Each card: original icon on the left, title, readable description, and current /
target progress where applicable. Unlocked cards show a completion mark/date;
locked cards stay readable with quieter icons. Do not rely on color alone.
Use restrained orange outlines, dark card fills and small rounded corners.
Select a card for its full condition, eligibility notes and larger artwork.
Make descriptions wrap and card heights adapt to translated text; no clipped
requirements or mandatory tiny fonts. Every title and description gets a token.
Support mouse wheel scrolling, keyboard navigation and Escape/Back consistently.
Keep the page available while disconnected, with cached progress where supported.
Progress should not reset because the player changes HUD presets or language.
Unlock notifications should be brief, with independent animation/sound controls.

Progress ownership and Steam integration still need a separate implementation
decision. Gameplay conditions should be evaluated from server-observed events;
a local preview must never be mistaken for an authoritative unlock. Community
server trust is a separate question: a mod-controlled server is not automatically
a trusted competitive authority simply because it sends an achievement event.

Reserve namespaced achievement IDs for this mod. Register and publish Steam
achievements only under our own assigned AppID, not the temporary SDK/HL2DM AppID.
Any development-only progress should remain separate from final Steam unlocks.

After the mod receives its own AppID and matchmaking support is ready: finalize
the launch set and conditions, then implement the main-menu entry, page, tracking
and original icons. Do not implement achievements before that milestone.
